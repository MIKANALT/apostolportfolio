COMPLETED · SCHOOL CAPSTONE

AgriConnect

AgriConnect is a web and mobile platform that connects local farmers in Bukidnon directly to buyers. Farmers can post agricultural products with price, quantity, and location, while buyers can browse, search, and contact farmers directly.

It aims to remove middlemen and increase farmer income.

Role: Database Manager / Back-end Developer

Handled: MySQL database design, farmer and buyer registration, product posting module, and system testing.
